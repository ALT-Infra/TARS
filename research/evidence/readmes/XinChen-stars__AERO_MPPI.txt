## Overview

**AERO-MPPI** is a **GPU-accelerated trajectory planner for quadrotors** based on the **Model Predictive Path Integral (MPPI)** framework.

Key features:

* 🛠️ **Native integration with Isaac Sim / Isaac Lab**
* 🎯 **Anchor-guided trajectory ensembles** for escaping local minima
* ⚡ **Warp-accelerated MPPI enabling thousands of trajectory rollouts across 1000+ parallel environments**
